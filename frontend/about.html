Our Roots

THE STREETS ARE
OUR STADIUM

Verse Jersey was founded in 2022 by a collective of players, designers, and season ticket holders. We grew tired of generic sportswear templates that lacked identity, soul, and custom expression.

We believe the jersey is more than a kit—it is a banner. It carries the weight of history, the pulse of the terrace chants, and the unique identity of the fan wearing it. That is why we built a premium workspace that puts high-fidelity printing and customization directly in your hands.

Our Mission

"To equip football purists and casual creators with bespoke, high-performance jerseys that blend stadium pedigree with streetwear relevance."

History Roadmap

OUR BRAND TIMELINE

November 2022

The Spark / Qatar World Cup

Founded in London during the winter World Cup. A group of designers launched a small batch of custom hand-printed retro jerseys that sold out in minutes, revealing a deep fan desire for customized expression.

March 2024

Customizer Studio Launch

Launched our futuristic customization platform, allowing users to enter custom details and preview them instantly in vector formats. Standardized global size selectors and sleeve patch upgrades.

May 2026

World Cup 2026 Collection

Officially branded as **Verse Jersey**, expanding our digital collection to cover club integrations, retro edits, goalkeeper kits, training zip-ups, and global delivery channels to 150+ countries.
