quiz Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to shipping, return policies, and nameset personalization.

Personalized jerseys with custom names and numbers require 1-2 business days for precision heat-pressed matching vinyl decals before shipping. Once customization is completed, standard shipping timelines apply.
Standard, non-customized jerseys can be returned inside 30 days in brand new condition with original tags. Personalized name/number shirts are custom manufactured and are final sale (no returns/exchanges) unless there is a manufacturer printing defect.
Free shipping is automatically triggered in the checkout cart for all orders totaling $150 or more. For orders below $150, a standard shipping fee of $9.99 applies.
We support Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and major cryptocurrencies. All transactions are securely processed through 256-bit SSL encrypted connections to guarantee complete security.
